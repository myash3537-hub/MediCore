About Our Hospital

THE SR'S HOSPITAL AND TRAUMA CENTRE was established with a mission to provide world‑class medical care to every patient. We combine advanced technology with a warm, human touch.

From critical trauma care to routine check‑ups, our experienced team of doctors, nurses, and support staff work together to ensure the best outcomes.

We pride ourselves on being one of the region's leading healthcare providers, offering state‑of‑the‑art equipment, modern operation theatres, and dedicated ICUs.
